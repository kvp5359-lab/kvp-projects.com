Dictum

Dictation into any app

Press a key, speak, and the text appears where your cursor is. Speech recognition right on your Mac or in the cloud with your own key, with AI text cleanup.

Coming soonmacOS 15 or later

Features

  • Recognition on your Mac (Whisper, Parakeet, Apple) or in the cloud: OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, ElevenLabs
  • Modes, each with its own model, language and AI text processing
  • A live pill with the text as you speak, pause and switch modes on the fly
  • A dictionary with word forms, dictation history by day
  • Music is lowered or paused while you record

The change history is available in Russian.
