LanguageApp Beta

Learn a language from what you say and read yourself

Record your speech or paste a text, and the app translates it, picks out words and phrases and turns them into your own course: a dictionary with reviews, drills, games, reading with translation and a conversation with an AI partner.

Download for AndroidVersion 0.6.9 · Android 13 or later · Interface: Russian only

Beta: the app is in active development, more is on the way.

Features

  • Speech recording and recognition right on the phone, phrases and words picked out
  • A dictionary with spaced reviews, topic groups and “Words on a topic”
  • Drills and games: Blaster, Race, Trail, Build the word
  • Reading books and texts with pages translated ahead and words on a tap
  • A translator with voice and a spoken conversation with an AI partner

LanguageApp for Android

To install: press “Download for Android” on the phone and open the downloaded LanguageApp-….apk file. Android asks once to allow installing from the browser: “Settings” → “Allow from this source”, then “Back” and “Install”. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, press “More details” → “Install anyway”: the app isn’t on Google Play, so the phone doesn’t know it.

Updates come from within the app: it downloads the new version from this site and checks the file’s signature before installing. Android asks you to confirm the install.

To uninstall: Settings → Apps → LanguageApp → Uninstall. Android removes the data on the phone along with the app; what’s synced stays in your kvp account.
