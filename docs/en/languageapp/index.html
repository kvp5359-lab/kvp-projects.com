LanguageApp Beta

Learn a language from what you say and read yourself

Record your speech or paste a text, and the app translates it, picks out words and phrases and turns them into your own course: a dictionary with reviews, drills, games, reading with translation and a conversation with an AI partner.

Download for AndroidVersion 0.1.17 · Android 13 or later · Interface: Russian only

Beta: the app is in active development, more is on the way.

Features

  • Speech recording and recognition right on the phone, phrases and words picked out
  • A dictionary with spaced reviews, topic groups and “Words on a topic”
  • Drills and games: Blaster, Race, Trail, Build the word
  • Reading books and texts with pages translated ahead and words on a tap
  • A translator with voice and a spoken conversation with an AI partner

What’s new on Android

Version 0.1.17 · November 15, 2026

Added

  • A new game in Games, Trail: a board game on an island. Roll the die and walk the path through the beach, forest, swamp and mountains to the castle with the treasure. Every cell has a task with your words: pick the translation, recognise the word by ear or put it together from letters. Right — a star; wrong — a step back. A bridge carries you over the river, a trap throws you further back, and a guard waits at the castle. The game is saved; the record is the fewest rolls.
  • Translator: a Translator tile on Home, also opened by long-pressing the app icon. Type or say a phrase in your native or learned language, and the direction switches by itself. You get the translation read aloud, other ways to say it (more polite, casual) and the phrase's words in their dictionary form. Add the phrase or any word to the dictionary with one tap. Without the AI, the phone's translator gives the translation alone.
  • Words for a topic: in the Dictionary, describe a topic, such as “at the doctor's, stomach ache”, and the AI picks words and set phrases for it at your level. Tick the ones you need: they go to the dictionary as that topic's group, and you can learn them right away. Don't like the pick? Tap More. Topics are kept.
  • The Dictionary can show one topic's group: the list and Learn cover only it.

Changed

  • The Text button is gone from Home: to read your own text, paste it in the Reading tab.

LanguageApp for Android

To install: press “Download for Android” on the phone and open the downloaded LanguageApp-….apk file. Android asks once to allow installing from the browser: “Settings” → “Allow from this source”, then “Back” and “Install”. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, press “More details” → “Install anyway”: the app isn’t on Google Play, so the phone doesn’t know it.

Updates come from within the app: it downloads the new version from this site and checks the file’s signature before installing. Android asks you to confirm the install.

To uninstall: Settings → Apps → LanguageApp → Uninstall. Android removes the data on the phone along with the app; what’s synced stays in your kvp account.
