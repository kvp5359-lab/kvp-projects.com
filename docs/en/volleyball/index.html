Волейбол Beta

Keep the score on your phone, the stats add up by themselves

For friends who play together: mark who came, split into teams and keep the score point by point. Players’ points, wins and sets add up by themselves and everyone in the group sees them.

Download for AndroidVersion 0.3.2 · Android 13 or later · Interface: Russian only

Beta: the app is in active development, more is on the way.

Features

  • Game day: who came, teams by hand or at random, repeat the last teams
  • Score with one tap: a point to a player, the team or for the other side’s error; who serves, set and match point
  • Substitutions and serve fixes during a match, “Finish” at the current score
  • Beach or indoor with flexible rules: points per set, number of sets
  • Players’ stats for the day, month, year and all time
  • A group on several phones: join with a code, a match on another phone is shown live

Волейбол for Android

To install: press “Download for Android” on the phone and open the downloaded Volleyball-….apk file. Android asks once to allow installing from the browser: “Settings” → “Allow from this source”, then “Back” and “Install”. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, press “More details” → “Install anyway”: the app isn’t on Google Play, so the phone doesn’t know it.

Updates come from within the app: it downloads the new version from this site and checks the file’s signature before installing. Android asks you to confirm the install.

To uninstall: Settings → Apps → Волейбол → Uninstall. Android removes the data on the phone along with the app; what’s synced stays in your kvp account.
