Note Calc

Notes that do the math

macOS

Write as in a notepad, and lines with calculations show the result right away: variables, currencies with today's rates, percentages, dates and mini tables.

Coming soonRequires macOS 10.13 or later
  • Variables, line references and total words: sum, average, total
  • Functions in English and Russian, currencies, percentages, taxes, dates
  • Mini tables with formulas and totals
  • Notes are plain Markdown files in Documents
  • Quick entry from any app with ⌘⇧Space

Free. Your texts, recordings and files stay with you.

What’s new

Version 0.2.0 · 2026-10-10

Added

  • A What's New window after an update, and the whole history of changes — in the Note Calc menu → “What's New…” and in Settings → Help.
  • Error reports, only with your consent: if something breaks, the developer gets a technical report (version, system, where the error happened). Your notes, calculations and files are never sent. Turn them on in Settings → General; the app asks once by itself.
Earlier versions

Version 0.1.0 · 2026-10-10

Added

  • Notes that calculate by themselves: write as in a notepad, lines with calculations show the result on the right or right after the line.
  • Variables and line references: “Price = 15”, reference chips with @; renaming a line updates every reference.
  • Total words: sum, average, prev, total — they add up the block above or the whole note.
  • Functions in Russian and English (СУММ / SUM, ОКРУГЛ / ROUND, ЕСЛИ / IF…), currencies with today's rates, percentages and taxes, dates.
  • Mini tables with formulas, a total row and column, filling formulas across.
  • Select lines or cells — the sum, average and count appear at the bottom.
  • Notes are plain Markdown files in Documents, groups are folders; tabs, a sidebar list with search, pinning and trash.
  • Images and files in notes: paste, drag from Finder, preview like in Finder with Space.
  • Quick entry from any app with a shortcut (⌘⇧Space) — into the Inbox or into a chosen note.
  • A menu bar icon, a window above all windows, a window along the right edge of the screen at full height.
  • Russian and English interface, light and dark theme, zoom, your own shortcuts.
