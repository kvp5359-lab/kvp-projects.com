Note Calc Beta

Write a note, and it does the math

New in 0.5.4 for MacA line added with Quick Entry to the open note shows up in the text right away: the note doesn't reload, the cursor and ⌘Z undo stay where they were.Oct 29 →

Write as in a notepad, and lines with calculations show the result right away: variables, currencies with today's rates, percentages, dates and mini tables.

Download for MacVersion 0.5.4 · macOS 15 or later
Download for AndroidVersion 0.8.5 · Android 13 or later

Beta: the app is in active development, more is on the way.

What you get

Do the math right in the textWrite as in a notepad, and lines with calculations show the result at once — no separate calculator.
Currencies, percentages and datesToday’s exchange rates, percentages, taxes, dates and mini tables with totals — in plain words, in English or Russian.
Your notes stay yoursThey are plain Markdown files in Documents: they open anywhere and aren’t locked into the app.

Features

  • Variables, line references and total words: sum, average, total
  • Functions in English and Russian, currencies, percentages, taxes, dates
  • Mini tables with formulas and totals
  • Notes are plain Markdown files in Documents
  • Quick entry from any app with ⌘⇧Space

All Note Calc updates

How to uninstall on Mac

In the app: Note Calc menu → «Uninstall Note Calc…» (or Settings → Help). Removes the app, its settings and license, and its access to Documents. Your notes in Documents → Note Calc stay — they’re yours.

Planning to come back? Tick «Keep my data» — install Note Calc again and everything will be there.

This is in version 0.5.0 and later.

By hand (for older versions)

Open Terminal, paste the line and press Return. It quits Note Calc and deletes it along with its data. The line is for zsh/bash (the standard Terminal). The app goes to the Trash via Finder — macOS asks once whether Terminal may control Finder.

osascript -e 'quit app "Note Calc"' 2>/dev/null; sleep 2; rm -rf ~/Library/{WebKit,"Application Support",Caches,HTTPStorages,Logs}/com.kvp.notecalc ~/Library/HTTPStorages/com.kvp.notecalc.binarycookies ~/Library/Preferences/com.kvp.notecalc.plist ~/Library/"Saved Application State"/com.kvp.notecalc.savedState; defaults delete com.kvp.notecalc 2>/dev/null; tccutil reset All com.kvp.notecalc; osascript -e 'tell application "Finder" to delete POSIX file "/Applications/Note Calc.app"' >/dev/null 2>&1 || echo "Drag Note Calc to the Trash yourself"

Note Calc for Android

To install: press “Download for Android” on the phone and open the downloaded Note-Calc-….apk file. Android asks once to allow installing from the browser: “Settings” → “Allow from this source”, then “Back” and “Install”. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, press “More details” → “Install anyway”: the app isn’t on Google Play, so the phone doesn’t know it.

Updates come from within the app: it downloads the new version from this site and checks the file’s signature before installing. Android asks you to confirm the install.

To uninstall: Settings → Apps → Note Calc → Uninstall. Android removes the data on the phone along with the app; what’s synced stays in your kvp account.
