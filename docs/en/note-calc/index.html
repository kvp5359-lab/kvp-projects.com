Note Calc Beta

Notes that do the math

Write as in a notepad, and lines with calculations show the result right away: variables, currencies with today's rates, percentages, dates and mini tables.

Download for MacVersion 0.5.2 · macOS 15 or later

Beta: the app is in active development, more is on the way.

Features

  • Variables, line references and total words: sum, average, total
  • Functions in English and Russian, currencies, percentages, taxes, dates
  • Mini tables with formulas and totals
  • Notes are plain Markdown files in Documents
  • Quick entry from any app with ⌘⇧Space

Version 0.5.0 · 2026-10-12

Changed

  • The formatting bar has a new look, like in Google Docs: buttons sit in rounded groups and show icons instead of letters. Clicking «A» colors the text with the color picked last (shown by the bar under the letter); the arrow next to it opens the palette. Scrolled text fades out under the bar. In a narrow window the bar stays on one line: buttons that do not fit go under «⋯».
  • The interface has no thin dividing lines anymore: the sidebar, tabs, totals bar, settings and input fields are set apart by soft fills and rounded corners.

Added

  • You can remove Note Calc from your Mac right inside the app: Note Calc menu → «Uninstall Note Calc…» or Settings → Help. The app goes to the Trash along with its settings and license (the «Keep my data» checkbox keeps them). Your notes in Documents → Note Calc stay.
  • The formatting bar now has underline (⌘U), text color (the «A» button, seven colors that adapt to the dark theme), highlight (⌘⇧H) and «Clear formatting» (⌘\): it takes bold, color, headings, lists and the rest off the selection, links stay. Shortcuts can be changed in Settings.
  • Find and replace within a note: ⌘F, ⌘G for the next match, ⌘⇧G for the previous one.
  • Spell checking: misspelled words are underlined, as in other Mac apps. Lines with calculations are not checked.
  • With the cursor on a link, its address shows under it: edit it, open the link or remove it and keep the text.
  • Dashes and quotes as you type: "--" with a space becomes "—", straight quotes become “curly” ones after English text and «guillemets» after Russian. ⌘Z right after brings back what you typed.
  • Brackets close themselves: type "(" and ")" appears too.
  • --- on its own line is drawn as a dividing line.
  • Shift+Enter in a list item starts a new line within the same item instead of a new item. Backspace at the start of that line joins it back.
  • Formatting survives copy and paste: bold, italics, headings, lists, checkboxes and links pasted from a browser, Google Docs, Word, Notes or chats stay formatted. What you copy from Note Calc arrives formatted in mail, documents and messengers, and as clean text without asterisks where there's no formatting.

Fixed

  • Text in square brackets no longer looks like a link and keeps its brackets.
  • Pasted text is no longer selected as a whole just because it mentions numbers: only a pasted column of numbers is selected (and summed right away).
  • Blank lines are kept when copying into message boxes and similar fields, where paragraphs used to run together.
  • The line above --- no longer turns into a big heading.
  • Enter on an empty list item leaves the list instead of adding a blank line. Backspace at the start of an item removes its bullet (or moves a nested item one level out).
  • Enter in the middle of a bold (italic, etc.) word keeps both halves formatted.
  • Text typed right after a bold word is bold too, as in Word and Google Docs. To go on in plain text, press ⌘B at the end of the word.
  • A numbered list renumbers itself when you delete, paste or move an item: delete the third item and the fourth becomes the third.

How to uninstall

In the app: Note Calc menu → «Uninstall Note Calc…» (or Settings → Help). Removes the app, its settings and license, and its access to Documents. Your notes in Documents → Note Calc stay — they’re yours.

Planning to come back? Tick «Keep my data» — install Note Calc again and everything will be there.

This is in version 0.5.0 and later.

By hand (for older versions)

Open Terminal, paste the line and press Return. It quits Note Calc and deletes it along with its data. The line is for zsh/bash (the standard Terminal). The app goes to the Trash via Finder — macOS asks once whether Terminal may control Finder.

osascript -e 'quit app "Note Calc"' 2>/dev/null; sleep 2; rm -rf ~/Library/{WebKit,"Application Support",Caches,HTTPStorages,Logs}/com.kvp.notecalc ~/Library/HTTPStorages/com.kvp.notecalc.binarycookies ~/Library/Preferences/com.kvp.notecalc.plist ~/Library/"Saved Application State"/com.kvp.notecalc.savedState; defaults delete com.kvp.notecalc 2>/dev/null; tccutil reset All com.kvp.notecalc; osascript -e 'tell application "Finder" to delete POSIX file "/Applications/Note Calc.app"' >/dev/null
