Privacy

In short: my apps work on your computer and don’t collect anything about you. Below is everything they send over the internet, and why.

Your data stays on your computer

Notes, clipboard history, screenshots and screen recordings, dictations, texts and settings are kept only on your computer. I can’t see them and don’t upload them anywhere.

Error reports — if you want

When something breaks, an app can send a report so I can find and fix the bug. Reports are sent only with your consent: the app asks on first launch, and you can turn them off in its settings at any time.

On a Mac, a report contains the app and macOS versions, details about the Mac (processor, memory, free space) and technical notes about what happened before the error. It never includes your texts, dictations, files, screenshots or keys; the path to your home folder is replaced with “~”.

Reports go through my server reports.kvp-projects.com to Sentry (data stored in the EU). My server doesn’t pass your IP address on, and Sentry is set not to store IP addresses.

Updates and licenses

Once a day an app checks for a new version and reads the license policy file on updates.kvp-projects.com. It’s a plain file download: nothing about you or how you use the app is sent. Like any website, the server sees the address the request came from. A new version is installed only with your consent, and automatic checks can be turned off in settings. The app files themselves come from there too — my own server, which keeps no access log.

A license key contains the email it was issued to and is checked right on your computer, without contacting a server. In an activation link the key comes after the “#” sign — browsers never send that part of the address to a server.

The Windows versions work the same way: your data stays on the computer, error reports go only with your consent (they carry the Windows version and computer details, never captures, texts or file names), and updates are checked and downloaded from the same server. The app verifies each update’s signature itself before installing it.

What individual apps send

Dictum. By default speech is recognized right on your Mac; the models are downloaded once from Hugging Face. If you choose cloud recognition (OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, ElevenLabs) or AI text processing (Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq), the recording or text goes to the service you chose, with your own key and under its terms. Ollama, LM Studio and Apple’s model run on your Mac. Apple’s built-in recognition works under Apple’s terms.

Textum. AI actions send the selected text and the command to Anthropic (Claude) with your own key — or process it with Apple’s model right on your Mac. Currency conversion downloads exchange rates from open.er-api.com, without any of your data.

Note Calc. Downloads exchange rates for currencies from open.er-api.com, without any of your data.

Shotlark for Mac. A file name from the screenshot’s content is made by Apple’s model right on your Mac. Only if you turned on names from Claude in Settings and entered your own Anthropic key, and Apple’s model is missing or gave no answer, a small copy of the screenshot, the app and window names and the text recognized on it go to Anthropic (Claude) — just to name the file. Without that setting or a key nothing is sent.

Shotlark for Windows. A file name from the capture’s content is made right on your computer. Only if you turned on “Ask Claude for the name” in Settings and entered your own Anthropic key, a small copy of the capture (a JPEG of up to 1024 pixels), the app and window names and the text recognized on it go to Anthropic (Claude) — just to name the file. Without that setting or a key nothing is sent.

Cliplark sends nothing beyond what is described above.

Browser extensions

The extensions on this site have no error reports and no analytics. Settings are kept by Chrome itself; for Subtitle Tools and Steady Speed, if sync is on in Chrome, Chrome carries them between your devices through your Google account.

Subtitle Tools. Subtitles come from the video site itself (YouTube or kino.watch). Translation with Claude is off by default. Only if you turned it on and entered your own Anthropic key, the subtitle text and the video title go to Anthropic (Claude), with your key and under its terms. The key and finished translations are kept only in this browser. Chrome’s built-in translator is part of the browser and works under Chrome’s terms.

Steady Speed and Clear Site Data send nothing over the internet.

This website

No analytics, ads or cookies; fonts are served by the site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages — like any host, GitHub sees technical request data such as the IP address.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

On your computer data stays until you delete it. How to remove an app along with it, and what stays, is on each app’s page.

On my server (api.kvp-projects.com) only people whose key was issued or bought through it are stored. Pro isn’t on sale yet: for now these are keys I issue myself, for vpn-app for example. Stored: the email the key is issued to, the license number, plans and the day they’re paid through, usage counters by month; for vpn-app also a device’s id and name, when it first and last connected, and a person’s name if I noted it; a service log of these actions. There’s no automatic deadline: it all stays until I delete the record at your request. The VPN panel’s record has no name or email.

In Supabase my server keeps a copy of your email and your rights with their dates: sync will open by them. Sync itself is still off in released apps.

Copies of the database. Every night the server copies this database and keeps the last 14, on the same server. Copies aren’t edited: a deleted record leaves them as they’re erased in turn, normally within 14 days.

Server logs. No access log is kept at updates., reports., api. and sync.kvp-projects.com. The service writes only the kind of an error, without keys or emails; this log is at most 30 MB, and old entries are erased by themselves.

Error reports are kept in Sentry according to Sentry’s settings: I haven’t set a period of my own.

How to delete. If you’d like me to delete data about you, write to me: kvp5359 at gmail.com I’ll delete your email, plans, usage, devices and log entries from the server, take away your rights in Supabase and switch off your access in the VPN panel (a switched-off record without a name or email stays there). I’ll revoke the issued key so that it stops opening the apps; after that nothing stays on the server except the copies from the paragraph above, until they’re erased.
