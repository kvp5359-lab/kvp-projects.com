Clear Site Data Beta

Clear data for one site

Cache, cookies and storage for the site in the current tab only — other sites stay untouched.

Coming soon to Chrome Web Store

Beta: the app is in active development, more is on the way.

Features

  • Clear the cache, cookies or everything
  • Protection against accidentally logging out
  • See how many cookies the site has and on which domains
  • Alt+Shift+C clears the cache and reloads the tab

The change history is available in Russian.
