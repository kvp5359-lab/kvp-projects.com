Привычки Beta

Do what you promised yourself — every day

Habits on any schedule, with a time and a duration, reminders and a calendar of their own beside Google Calendar. At the right moment the app can cover the screen like an alarm clock until a task is done: translate foreign words, solve a sum or shake the phone. A parent sees a child’s habits and results.

Download for AndroidVersion 0.2.1 · Android 13 or later · Interface: Russian only

Beta: the app is in active development, more is on the way.

Features

  • Every day, on weekdays, every few days, on days of the month or N times a week
  • “Don’t do” habits, several times a day, pauses and skips that keep the streak
  • Several reminders, like in Google Calendar, and “Done” right from the notification
  • A month / week / day calendar and habits in Google Calendar
  • A screen lock with a task and an emergency exit, statistics for each habit, family

Привычки for Android

To install: press “Download for Android” on the phone and open the downloaded habit-app-….apk file. Android asks once to allow installing from the browser: “Settings” → “Allow from this source”, then “Back” and “Install”. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, press “More details” → “Install anyway”: the app isn’t on Google Play, so the phone doesn’t know it.

Updates come from within the app: it downloads the new version from this site and checks the file’s signature before installing. Android asks you to confirm the install.

To uninstall: Settings → Apps → Привычки → Uninstall. Android removes the data on the phone along with the app; what’s synced stays in your kvp account.
