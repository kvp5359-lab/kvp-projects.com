Shotlark

Screenshots and screen recording

Capture an area, a window or the screen, scrolling captures, annotations, text recognition and screen recording to video or GIF.

Coming soonmacOS 26 or later

Features

  • Captures with a timer, frozen screen, crosshair and magnifier
  • Scrolling capture — one long image
  • Editor: arrows, shapes, text, steps, blur, crop
  • Text and QR code recognition in captures
  • Screen recording to video or GIF with sound and clicks

What’s new

Version 0.11.0 · 2026-10-10

Added

  • Error reports, only with your consent: if something breaks, the developer gets a technical report (Mac model, versions, app state, the work log). Your captures, recordings and file names never go into it. Turn them on at the first step of the welcome tour or in Settings → General.
  • What's New after an update, and the whole history of changes — in the menu bar icon's “What’s New…” and in Settings → About.
  • A log of the app's work — useful if something went wrong. Your file names and the contents of captures are never written into it.

Changed

  • Thicker lines: the five widths now go from 2.5 to 16 — for arrows, shapes, pencil and highlighter. Marks already drawn don't change.
  • The “Pencil” style looks more hand-drawn: almost sharp corners, an uneven line with varying width.
  • Settings and the welcome tour in a new look, like my other apps: cards, pill buttons, search finds single settings.
  • Hints and texts in Russian now speak to you informally.

Fixed

  • When the last window closes, the Dock icon goes away and the keyboard returns to the app you were working in.
Earlier versions

Version 0.10.55 · 2026-10-10

Added

  • Captures of an area, a window, the whole screen and the previous area, with a timer; while selecting, the screen freezes and you get a crosshair and a magnifier; every mode has its own shortcut.
  • Scrolling capture: select an area and scroll the page (or turn on auto-scroll) — you get one long image.
  • A thumbnail after each capture: copy, save, drag into any app, pin or open in the editor.
  • Annotation editor: arrows, shapes, text, pencil, highlighter, numbered steps, hiding (pixelate, blur), crop; two styles — “Classic” and “Pencil”.
  • “Image” mode in the editor: edit the capture itself — eraser, color picker with a magnifier; the original stays intact.
  • Pin a capture above all windows; double-click brings it back to the editor.
  • Text recognition on captures (Russian and English, QR codes).
  • Screen recording to video or GIF: an area, a window or the whole screen, computer sound and microphone, cursor and clicks, pause.
  • Video editor: trim the start and end, volume, save and export with compression.
  • Capture and recording history by day — with search and a retention period.
  • Hide desktop icons — by hand or just for the moment of a capture.
  • Settings with search, choose the actions in the menu bar icon's menu, shutter sound, PNG, JPG, HEIC and AVIF formats, a file name template.
  • A welcome tour on first launch: permissions, shortcuts, what happens after a capture.
