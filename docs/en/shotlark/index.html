Shotlark Beta

Screenshots and screen recording

Capture an area, a window or the screen, scrolling captures, annotations, text recognition and screen recording to video or GIF.

Download for MacVersion 0.13.0 · macOS 26 or later · Apple silicon

Beta: the app is in active development, more is on the way.

Features

  • Captures with a timer, frozen screen, crosshair and magnifier
  • Scrolling capture — one long image
  • Editor: arrows, shapes, text, steps, blur, crop
  • Text and QR code recognition in captures
  • Screen recording to video or GIF with sound and clicks

What’s new

How to uninstall

In the app: Shotlark menu → «Uninstall Shotlark…» (or Settings → About). Removes the app, its settings, capture history and editor projects, its Privacy permissions and the login item. Captures you saved to your own folders stay. Only a small shared folder, kvp-projects/licensing, stays: it remembers since when you’ve been with us and keeps your key, if you have one — for all my apps at once. None of your texts or files are in it.

Planning to come back? Tick «Keep my data and settings» — install Shotlark again and everything will be there.

This is in versions newer than 0.13.0.

By hand (for older versions)

Open Terminal, paste the line and press Return. It quits Shotlark and deletes it along with its data. The line is for zsh/bash (the standard Terminal). The app goes to the Trash via Finder — macOS asks once whether Terminal may control Finder.

osascript -e 'if application "Shotlark" is running then tell application "Shotlark" to quit'; sleep 2; osascript -e 'tell application "Finder" to delete POSIX file "/Applications/Shotlark.app"' >/dev/null 2>&1; rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/screenshot-app ~/Library/Logs/Shotlark ~/Library/Caches/com.kvp5359.screenshotapp ~/Library/Caches/Shotlark ~/Library/Caches/SentryCrash/Shotlark ~/Library/Caches/io.sentry/1307402e76817b842cc5779c4cb7f9f3c1c5706a ~/Library/HTTPStorages/com.kvp5359.screenshotapp ~/Library/HTTPStorages/com.kvp5359.screenshotapp.binarycookies ~/Library/WebKit/com.kvp5359.screenshotapp ~/Library/Saved\ Application\ State/com.kvp5359.screenshotapp.savedState; rmdir ~/Library/Caches/SentryCrash ~/Library/Caches/io.sentry 2>/dev/null; defaults delete com.kvp5359.screenshotapp 2>/dev/null; tccutil reset All com.kvp5359.screenshotapp
