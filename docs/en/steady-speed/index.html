Steady Speed Beta

Video speed that stays put

Video speed on any site: it doesn't reset after pausing or seeking and is remembered per site.

Coming soon to Chrome Web Store

Beta: the app is in active development, more is on the way.

Features

  • Speed holds after pause, seeking and full screen
  • Remembered per site, per tab or everywhere
  • Bookmarks in videos, named from the subtitles
  • Shortcuts that work in any keyboard layout

The change history is available in Russian.
